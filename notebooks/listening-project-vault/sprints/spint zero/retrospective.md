# what didn't work 
- tried to move too quickly past design problems Claude was raising instead of sitting with them. need to be more conscious of design and trace code paths much more carefully — carelessness caused most of the rework this sprint. 
## what worked 
- one-step-at-a-time pacing: resolving one concept/problem fully before moving to the next. - banging my head on a bug for a while BEFORE bringing it to Claude — turned out the bug had nothing to do with what i was looking at, but the struggle time was the right call. 
- making myself start every day. the 3hr/day minimum turned into genuine enjoyment once momentum kicked in — sessions regularly ran long because time started flying, not because i forced it. 
# broader note 
- may and june were badly slacked. the fix wasn't motivation, it was ritual: starting is the expensive part, and daily consistency pays that cost automatically. protect the daily-start habit over everything else.
# velocity 
- sprint zero: 4 working days, ~3hr sessions (variable), learning-heavy (~80% brand-new concepts: fastapi, decorators, import semantics, exception design, uv, secrets management). - concern: this pace feels slow. counterpoint: learning-heavy sprints don't recur — sprint one reuses everything this sprint taught. next sprint measures building-heavy velocity; the gap between the two numbers is the real calibration, so don't judge odds of progress until both exist.
